EMPLOYEE INFORMATION AND BENEFITS

Document Type: Human Resources Information
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Working Hours
The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a one-hour lunch break. Some teams may follow different schedules based on business requirements.

2. Remote Work
Eligible employees may work remotely according to their role and manager-approved schedule. Employees must remain reachable during their scheduled working hours and follow company security requirements.

3. Health Benefits
Eligible full-time employees may participate in the company's health-benefits program. Coverage details depend on the employee's plan and location.

4. Learning and Development
Employees may participate in approved training programs, technical workshops, and professional-development activities. Employees should discuss relevant opportunities with their manager.

5. Performance Reviews
Employees normally participate in periodic performance reviews. Reviews may consider job responsibilities, performance objectives, collaboration, and professional development.

6. Employee Responsibilities
Employees are expected to maintain accurate employment information, complete required training, follow company policies, and communicate workplace concerns through appropriate channels.

LEAVE POLICY

Document Type: Human Resources Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose
This policy describes the general leave process for employees.

2. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual paid leave per calendar year. Annual leave should normally be requested at least 5 working days before the planned start date.

3. Sick Leave
Employees may take up to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible when they are unable to work because of illness.

4. Emergency Leave
Emergency leave may be requested when an unexpected personal or family emergency requires an employee to be away from work. The employee should notify their manager as soon as possible.

5. Leave Approval
Leave requests must be submitted through the company's approved leave-management process. Approval depends on business requirements, available leave balance, and applicable policy rules.

6. Holidays
Company holidays are maintained in the official annual holiday calendar. The holiday calendar may vary by office location.

7. Policy Updates
Employees should consult the latest approved HR policy and holiday calendar because leave rules and holiday schedules may change.
